Understanding Stroke

Learn about the causes, symptoms, and risk factors associated with stroke.

What is a Stroke?

A stroke occurs when blood flow to the brain is blocked, leading to brain damage.

Common Symptoms

Symptoms include sudden numbness, confusion, trouble speaking, or severe headache.

Risk Factors

High blood pressure, smoking, heart_disease, and obesity increase stroke risk.
